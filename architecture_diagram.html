Enterprise High-Level Design (HLD)

Inactive Domain Discovery, Verification & Dataset Replacement Pipeline

Autonomous discovery of official web footprints for 30,420 inactive corporate records via 24 concurrent Apify search actors, multi-tier Scrapling TLS crawling, Elimination-First identity verification, and reconciliation into the 93,918-row master dataset.

Master Dataset Scope
93,918
Complete corporate entity records
Inactive Recovery Scope
30,420
Inactive domains targeted for discovery
Apify Search Concurrency
24 Workers
Parallel actors (50 queries/batch)
Scrapling Concurrency
150 Crawlers
TLS/curl-impersonate fingerprinting
